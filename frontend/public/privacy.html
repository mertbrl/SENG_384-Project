🛡️ Health AI — Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Health AI, developed by Pi-thon Dynamics. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure, trusted environment for collaboration between healthcare professionals and engineers. This policy outlines how we handle your data in compliance with GDPR standards.

2. Information We Collect

To maintain a verified and professional network, we collect:

  • Authentication data: Your institutional email address (.edu or .edu.tr) and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile information: Your selected platform role (Engineer or Healthcare Professional), domain of expertise, and location (city / country).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Platform access & security: To verify your academic or institutional identity, send verification emails, and manage your secure sessions via JWT authentication.
  • Smart match score: We process your role, domain, and location strictly to calculate collaboration match scores and connect you with relevant project partners.
  • System notifications: To deliver essential platform updates and project connection requests.

4. Data Security & NDA Framework

Your personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Furthermore, any direct project collaboration initiated through Health AI is protected by our mandatory Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) flow to help safeguard clinical context and engineering intellectual property shared in the platform workflow.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR)

You retain control over your personal information. Within your account settings, you have the right to:

  • Access and download a copy of your personal data (for example in JSON format), where the product supports export.
  • Update or correct your profile information.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your account and associated data, subject to lawful retention requirements.

By creating an account, you consent to the collection and use of your information as outlined in this policy, and to the platform rules described in the Terms of Service below.

📜 Health AI — Terms of Service
